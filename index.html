About us

Your local IT partner in Jorpati

Mansa Technology Pvt. Ltd. is an IT solutions company at the Jorpati–Sundarijal bus stop in Kathmandu. We install CCTV, set up networks and audio systems, handle electrical work, and repair computers and printers.

Over the years we've earned a name for dependable work and quality products, for homes and businesses alike. Our trained technicians install everything properly and stay on hand for support long after the job is done.

In store you'll find CCTV cameras, NVRs from Hikvision, Tiandy and Dahua, PoE switches, Cat6 cable, RJ45 and RJ11 connectors, multi-plugs and computer peripherals. Everything we sell is genuine, certified and covered by warranty.
